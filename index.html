Julie & Me

Palangga ko, my baby Julie,

Written with all the love my heart can hold

Baby, from the very moment you walked into my world, everything began to feel softer, brighter, and infinitely more meaningful. Sometimes I just pause and look at you, or think about your laugh, and my whole chest fills up with a peace I cannot put into ordinary words. You are not just someone I love, Julie; you are the most precious, radiant, and gentle soul I have ever known.
Every single night when I close my eyes, and every morning when I wake up, I thank God for you. Out of billions of people in this wide world, He guided our steps, wove our stories together, and allowed our paths to cross. I know with all my heart that meeting you was never an accident—it was God’s gracious, tender answered prayer in my life.
Thank you, my baby, for your sweetness, for your daily care, for the precious time you share with me, and for all the little quiet ways you show you love me. The cute messages you send, the gentle check-ins when you ask how my day went, the warmth in your voice—they brighten my heaviest days. Palangga ta gid ka, more than you will ever completely know.
Do you know one of my absolute favorite things in this world? It’s hearing you sing praises for God. When you sing, your voice carries so much purity, devotion, and grace. It softens my heart, it calms my mind, and it reminds me how beautiful your faith is. Seeing your genuine love for God makes me admire, honor, and cherish you on an even deeper level. You are truly a woman of God, and I am blessed beyond measure to stand by your side.
I still smile warmly whenever I remember the moment I met your mother. To be completely honest, baby, I was so shy and nervous that my hands were trembling a little! But inside, that moment meant the entire world to me. It showed me the warm, loving home you grew up in, and it made me want nothing more than to respect, protect, and honor you and the people you hold dear.
I treasure every single second we have ever spent together. Our dates, our late-night conversations where we lose track of hours, the warmth of holding your gentle hands, our shared jokes, and the quiet comfortable silences where we don't even need words. In your arms, I have found my safest haven, and with you, I can be completely, unapologetically myself.
I want you to always feel loved, deeply appreciated, respected, safe, and treasured. I never want you to doubt your worth or wonder if you are enough. To me, baby Julie, you are more than enough. You are my dream come true, my comfort, my greatest joy, and the girl I want to keep protecting with everything in me.
Life will not always be soft sunshine and gentle breezes. There will be busy weeks, hard seasons, misunderstandings, and tired days. But here is what I want you to hold onto: I don't just love you when it is easy; I choose you when it is hard. I choose to listen, I choose to be patient, I choose to hold your hand tighter, and I choose to stand right beside you through every single storm.
I want us to keep growing together, learning together, praying together, and chasing God's purpose for our lives hand-in-hand. I want to build a lifetime of memories with you—traveling to places we've dreamed of, cooking together, laughing until our stomachs hurt, and holding each other close at the end of long days.
I won’t make empty fairy-tale guarantees that I cannot keep. But I will make you these sincere, unshakable promises: I promise to love you with honesty and loyalty. I promise to be your biggest cheerleader and your quiet shelter. I promise to pray for you daily, to respect you always, and to never stop looking at you with the same awe and tenderness I feel right now.
Julie, my baby, I love you with real intention, patient grace, and a loyalty that does not waver. Whatever tomorrow holds, you will never have to face it alone. Paghalong perme, palangga ko. You are my home, my favorite blessing, and the only girl my heart will ever belong to.
